Most companies out there believe that the best Turing test we have is the Captcha, and honestly it isn't working.Suchintan Singh from Skyvern joins us to talk through the challenges of automating processes on the web.

Somehow we end up in the philosophical debate regarding one second after the discovery of human-proof-of-work, the CAPTCHAs. And if we can't solve these, you have to question, what is even a human?

We dive into why relying purely on APIs is a failing strategy for web automation: out of 1.1 billion websites, only a fraction have functional APIs, let alone usable. Most cost-center organizations such as government portals or local telecom billing sites, have zero incentive to build or maintain developer endpoints. This reality plunges us into the ethical ambiguity of bot deployment. We review why actively bans users attempting to build Reddit bot farms or mass-create Anthropic accounts is still problematic and blur lines when standard business automation triggers an organization's anti-bot defenses.

We get into the escalating absurdity of modern CAPTCHAs, noting how users are now forced to complete ridiculous mini-games—like solving dynamic platformer levels just to prove they aren't machines.
